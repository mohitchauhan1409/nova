Let’s hear you.

Nova needs microphone access for live conversations. Your browser remembers your choice for Nova across websites.

If you previously blocked Nova, change its microphone setting to Allow, or reset it to Ask to see the prompt again.

Choose the browser option to allow access while using Nova when available. A temporary “allow this time” choice may prompt again later.
